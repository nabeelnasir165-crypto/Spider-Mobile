# UK Mobile Repair Business System (Hybrid Repair Suite V1) - PRD & Wireframe Spec

## Overview
A comprehensive UK-based mobile repair business management system focused on operational efficiency, clear status tracking, and ease of use.

## Design Principles
- **Style**: Modern SaaS (Stripe/Shopify inspired).
- **Theme**: Light (default) with dark mode support.
- **Layout**: Card-based, clean typography, soft shadows, rounded corners.
- **UX**: Minimize clicks (max 2-3 per action), high readability, searchable.

## Screen List
1. **Login Screen**: Minimalist center card for authentication.
2. **Main Admin Dashboard**: KPI cards and active repairs overview.
3. **Create Repair Ticket**: Multi-step wizard for efficient intake.
4. **Ticket Details Page**: Detailed view with timeline and technician notes.
5. **Repairs List**: Filterable table of all repair jobs.
6. **Customer Management**: Split-view for customer profiles and history.
7. **Bookings Management**: Calendar and list views for scheduling.
8. **Pricing Manager**: Tree-structured pricing editor for brands/models.
9. **CMS Dashboard**: Grid of editor cards for the customer website.
10. **Homepage Editor (CMS)**: Section-based editor with live preview.
11. **Payment Management**: Financial summary and invoice tracking.
12. **Warranty Management**: Tracking active warranties and claims.
13. **Staff Management**: Role-based access and activity logs.
14. **Settings Page**: Business configuration and system preferences.
